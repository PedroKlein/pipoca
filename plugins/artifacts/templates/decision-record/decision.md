# Decision: the question, in one line

**Decides:** who makes the call · **Reviewers:** who reviews it · **By:** the date it's needed

## Context

What is true today, what forces a decision now, and what happens if nothing is decided. Link the issue, the metric or the code each claim comes from.

## Options

### Option A: a short name

What it is, in two or three sentences.

- **Costs:** the work, the people and the time it takes.
- **Makes easier:** what this unlocks.
- **Makes harder:** what it rules out or risks.

### Option C: change nothing

## Open questions

- A question that must be answered before deciding, and who can answer it.

## Decision

Written when the record is marked Decided: the option chosen, why, and what would make us look at it again.
